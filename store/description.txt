AudioRack puts a studio-style effect rack on any browser tab. Route the sound of a video, stream, podcast or music player through a chain of EQ, compressors, a limiter, filter, de-esser and bass booster, and watch it on live analyzers.

Tame videos that are too loud, make quiet dialogue easier to follow, take the harshness out of a voice, or give small laptop speakers more bass. Everything runs on your device, with no account and no uploads.

★ BUILD YOUR OWN CHAIN
• Add up to 16 effects and analyzers in any order.
• Drag to reorder, click to bypass. Bypassing crossfades, so there are no clicks or pops.
• Parallel groups: split the signal into up to four branches, each with its own effects, and mix them on a small mixer with faders, meters, pan, mute, solo and polarity. Great for parallel compression; branches are delay-compensated.
• A rack per tab: every tab starts from your shared rack and keeps its own changes, so a podcast can get a voice chain while music gets a bass-heavy one. Or let a tab follow the shared rack.
• Side panel: dock the rack next to the page you are listening to, and pin the effects you want to keep in view. Pinned effects stack in chain order, and picking one in the chain scrolls to it.
• A global bypass for quick before/after comparison.
• Input and output gain with level meters.

★ TEN EFFECTS
• Equalizer: up to 12 bands (bell, shelves, notch, band pass and 12–48 dB/oct cuts), dragged directly on top of a live spectrum.
• Compressor: soft knee, peak or RMS detection, sidechain high-pass, auto makeup and parallel mix, with a transfer curve and gain-reduction history.
• Limiter: a lookahead brickwall limiter that keeps the output below the ceiling you set.
• Multiband compressor: two to four bands on phase-aligned Linkwitz-Riley crossovers, each with its own settings, solo, mute and bypass.
• Gate: silences hum and hiss between phrases, with hold, hysteresis and a sidechain filter.
• Filter: resonant low pass, high pass, band pass or notch, with drive and a sweeping LFO.
• De-esser: softens harsh “s” sounds in speech and vocals, with a listen mode.
• Denoiser: reduces steady background noise such as hiss, hum and fan noise by following the noise floor, with a freeze and listen mode.
• Volume: gain, pan, stereo width and polarity, with a loudness meter.
• Bass Booster: a low boost plus added harmonics, so bass comes through even on small speakers.

★ FOUR ANALYZERS
• Spectrum: mono, left, right, mid or side, with peak hold, freeze and a frequency, note and level readout.
• Spectrogram: frequency over time, with adjustable levels and three color schemes.
• Stereo Image: a stereo scope next to the image by frequency, with phase correlation, balance and width meters.
• Loudness: an EBU R128 loudness meter with momentary, short-term and integrated loudness, loudness range and true peak, measured against a target such as -14 LUFS for streaming.
Analyzers pass the audio through untouched, so you can place one before and one after an effect to compare.

★ PRESETS
Start from built-in presets such as Night Mode, Voice Clarity, Bass Head, Parallel Punch, Loudness and Lo-Fi Radio, save your own (side panel pins included), and export or import them as files.

★ HOW TO USE
1. Open a tab that plays audio.
2. Click the AudioRack icon and press “Process tab”.
3. Adjust the rack. Use the side panel button to dock it next to the page, or the pop-out button for a larger window.
Press the button again or close the tab to stop. Chrome shows its usual sharing indicator on the tab while it is being processed.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All audio processing happens locally in your browser.
• Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
• No network requests, no analytics, no ads, no account.
• Only your rack and presets are stored, on your device.
