AudioRack puts a studio effect rack on any browser tab. Run the sound of a video, stream, podcast, call or music player through a chain of EQ, compressors, a limiter, gate, denoiser, de-esser and bass booster, and watch the result on live analyzers and a loudness meter.

Turn down videos that are too loud, bring up quiet dialogue, clean hiss and hum out of a recording, soften a harsh voice, or give small laptop speakers more bass. Everything runs on your device, with no account and no uploads.

★ BUILD YOUR OWN CHAIN
• Up to 16 effects and analyzers, in any order.
• Drag to reorder, click to bypass. Bypass crossfades, so there are no clicks or pops, and timing never shifts.
• Parallel groups: split the signal into up to four branches with their own effects, and mix them on a small mixer with faders, meters, pan, mute, solo and polarity. Branches are delay-compensated, which makes parallel compression easy.
• A rack per tab: each tab starts from your shared rack and keeps its own changes, so a podcast can get a voice chain while music gets a bass-heavy one. Or switch a tab to follow the shared rack.
• Side panel: dock the rack next to the page you are listening to and pin the effects you want in view. Pinned effects stack in chain order.
• Global bypass for instant before/after comparison, plus input and output gain with level meters.

★ TEN EFFECTS
• Equalizer: up to 12 bands (bell, shelves, notch, band pass and 12–48 dB/oct cuts), dragged right on top of a live spectrum.
• Compressor: soft knee, peak or RMS detection, sidechain high-pass, auto makeup and a parallel mix knob, with a transfer curve and gain-reduction history.
• Limiter: a lookahead brickwall that keeps the output under the ceiling you set.
• Multiband compressor: two to four bands on phase-aligned crossovers, each with its own settings, solo, mute and bypass.
• Gate: silences hum and hiss between phrases or tightens drums, with hold, hysteresis and a sidechain filter.
• Filter: resonant low pass, high pass, band pass or notch, with drive and a sweeping LFO.
• De-esser: softens harsh “s” sounds in speech and vocals, with a listen mode.
• Denoiser: reduces steady noise such as hiss, hum and fans. Let it follow the noise floor by itself, or play a few seconds of noise alone, press “Learn noise” and it removes exactly that profile. Listen mode lets you hear what it takes out.
• Volume: gain, pan, stereo width and per-channel polarity, with a loudness bar.
• Bass Booster: a low boost plus generated harmonics, so bass comes through even on small speakers.

★ FOUR ANALYZERS
• Spectrum: mono, left, right, mid or side, with peak hold, freeze and a frequency, note and level readout.
• Spectrogram: frequency over time, with adjustable levels and three color schemes.
• Stereo Image: a stereo scope next to the image by frequency, with phase correlation, balance and width meters.
• Loudness: an EBU R128 meter with momentary, short-term and integrated loudness, loudness range and true peak, against a target such as −14 LUFS for streaming or −23 LUFS for broadcast.
Analyzers leave the audio untouched, so you can put one before and one after an effect to compare.

★ PRESETS
Start from built-in presets such as Night Mode, Voice Clarity, Bass Head, Parallel Punch, Loudness and Lo-Fi Radio. Save your own, side panel pins and learned noise profiles included, and export or import them as files.

★ HOW TO USE
1. Open a tab that plays audio.
2. Click the AudioRack icon and press “Process tab”.
3. Adjust the rack. Dock it in the side panel, or pop it out into a larger window.
Press the button again or close the tab to stop. Chrome shows its usual sharing indicator on the tab while it is processed.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All audio is processed locally in your browser.
• Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
• No network requests, analytics, ads or account.
• Only your racks and presets are stored, on your device.
